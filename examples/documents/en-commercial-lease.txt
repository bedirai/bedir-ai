COMMERCIAL LEASE: EXTRACT AND FILE NOTES (synthetic sample)

Parties: Harbor Properties Ltd. (Landlord) and Lumen Coffee (Tenant). Premises: ground-floor unit, 14 Mill Street. Term: 1 March 2025 to 28 February 2028.

Clause 7.1. The Landlord shall keep the roof, structure and main water pipes in good repair.

Clause 7.2. If a defect covered by clause 7.1 makes any part of the Premises unusable, the Tenant shall notify the Landlord in writing. If the Landlord fails to complete such repairs within thirty (30) days of the notice, the Tenant may terminate this Lease without penalty.

Clause 12.1. Except as provided in clause 7.2, if the Tenant ends this Lease before the end of the Term, the Tenant shall pay a penalty equal to three (3) months' rent.

Clause 15.3. Notice of termination shall be given in writing and delivered by registered mail or by hand.

File notes
4 June 2026: Heavy rain. Water enters through the roof into the seating area. The Tenant closes the seating area, about 40% of the floor space.
5 June 2026: The Tenant emails the Landlord's property manager with photos and asks for an urgent roof repair. The manager replies the same day: "Received, we will send a roofer."
19 June 2026: A roofer inspects and patches part of the roof. Water leaks in again during rain on 2 July.
10 July 2026: The Landlord states that the roof was substantially repaired on 19 June and that the remaining damp is caused by the Tenant's ventilation.
14 July 2026: An independent surveyor hired by the Tenant reports active leaks at two points of the roof and finds no ventilation fault.
20 July 2026: The Tenant emails the property manager: "We are terminating the lease under clause 7.2." No registered letter is on file.
28 July 2026: The Landlord demands three months' rent as a penalty under clause 12.1.
