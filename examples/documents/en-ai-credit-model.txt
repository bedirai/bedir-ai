MODEL RISK REVIEW: SUMMARY (synthetic sample)

Model: NovaScore v3, a gradient-boosted credit-scoring model from an external vendor. Intended use: approve or decline personal loans up to 50,000.

Performance
On 48,000 historical applications from 2023 and 2024, NovaScore v3 reached an AUC of 0.81, compared with 0.74 for the bank's current scorecard.
At the same approval rate, projected defaults fall by 14%.

Fairness
Approval rates for applicants aged 18 to 25 are 11 percentage points lower than under the current scorecard.
The vendor states that age is not an input, but the model uses length of credit history, which is strongly correlated with age.

Explainability
The vendor provides reason codes for each decision. In a sample of 200 declined applications, reviewers found the main reason code unclear in 37 cases.

Operations
The vendor will not share training data or model weights. Monitoring would rely on monthly reports from the vendor.
The licence for the current scorecard expires in nine months.

Regulation
Under the bank's internal policy, any model used for credit decisions must allow independent validation of its training data.
Credit scoring of individuals is classified as high-risk under the EU AI Act, with obligations for both providers and deployers.

Reviewer's recommendation. Conditional approval, subject to an independent fairness audit and contractual access to the training data before go-live.
